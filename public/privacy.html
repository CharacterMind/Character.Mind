Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Overview

Character Mind Playtime Co ("we," "us," or "our") operates Character.Mind. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the service, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

From your Google account (at sign-in):

  • Your name and profile picture
  • Your Google email address
  • A unique Google account identifier

From your use of the service:

  • Conversation history: Messages exchanged with AI characters are stored server-side to maintain continuity across sessions.
  • Recent chats: A list of characters you have recently chatted with, stored to populate your sidebar.
  • Usage data: Token counts, daily call counts, daily read-aloud counts, session start times, and weekly usage totals, used to enforce plan limits and display your usage dashboard.
  • Subscription tier: Your current plan (Free, Advanced, X20, or X50), stored in our database.
  • User-created characters: Any characters you create, including their name, description, system prompt, and associated image.

Automatically collected:

  • Session identifiers (stored in a browser cookie, used for authentication)
  • Basic server logs (request timestamps, error messages) for operational purposes

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session
  • To deliver AI character responses tailored to your conversation history
  • To enforce and display your subscription plan limits
  • To populate your recent chats sidebar
  • To process subscription payments via PayPal
  • To send service-related emails (such as policy updates or important account notices) to the email address associated with your Google account
  • To detect and prevent abuse or violations of our Terms of Service

4. Third-Party Services

We share data with the following third parties only as necessary to operate the service:

  • Google OAuth 2.0 — handles sign-in. We receive your basic profile information as described above. Google's privacy policy applies to data processed by Google.
  • Groq — our AI inference provider. Your conversation messages are sent to Groq's API to generate character responses. Groq's privacy policy applies to data they process.
  • Neon (PostgreSQL) — our database host, where user data, conversations, and character definitions are stored.
  • Render — our hosting provider, where the application server runs.
  • PayPal — payment processing for paid subscriptions. PayPal's privacy policy applies to payment data they collect.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Data Retention

  • Conversation history is retained while your account is active and may be cleared at any time from within the app.
  • Usage data (daily/weekly counters) is retained for the current billing and limit window, then reset.
  • Account data (name, email, subscription tier) is retained as long as your account exists.
  • If you request account deletion, we will remove your personal data from our database within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

6. Email Communications

By creating an account, you agree to receive service-related emails such as important policy updates and account notices. These are not marketing emails. We do not send promotional emails without your explicit opt-in.

To stop receiving service emails, contact us at support.charactermind@gmail.com. Note that opting out of service emails may mean you miss important updates about your account or the platform.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure session management, and access controls on our database. However, no system is perfectly secure. You use the service at your own risk, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Character.Mind is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will delete it promptly. If you believe this has occurred, contact us at support.charactermind@gmail.com.

9. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and correction: You can view and update your profile picture within the app.
  • Conversation deletion: You can clear your conversation history with any character at any time from within the app.
  • Account deletion: To request full deletion of your account and data, email us at support.charactermind@gmail.com.
  • Data portability: Contact us if you would like a copy of the data we hold about you.
If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or California, additional rights may apply to you under GDPR or CCPA. Contact us at support.charactermind@gmail.com to exercise those rights.

10. Cookies

We use a single session cookie to keep you logged in. This cookie is strictly necessary for the service to function and does not track you across other websites. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date above and notify users via the platform. Continued use of Character.Mind after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data? Contact us at support.charactermind@gmail.com.
