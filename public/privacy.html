Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Overview

Character Mind Playtime Co ("we," "us," or "our") operates Character.Mind. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the service, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

From your Google account (at sign-in):

  • Your name and profile picture
  • Your Google email address
  • A unique Google account identifier

From your use of the service:

  • Active conversation history: Messages exchanged with AI characters are stored server-side to maintain continuity across sessions. You can clear this at any time from within the app.
  • Archived past conversations: When you start a new chat with a character, the previous conversation is automatically saved to your archive so you can browse it later through the History panel. Archived conversations are stored in our database and are included in account deletion requests.
  • Long-term memory notes: When you chat with Opys, we keep a short, automatically written summary of the earlier part of that chat so the character can remember it. It is stored with your account, used only for that chat, and deleted when you start a new chat or clear it.
  • Data kept in your browser: To load quickly, your device keeps a copy of your recent chats, past chats, profile picture, display name and preferences in your browser's local storage. Signing out does not erase it. To remove it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Recent chats: A list of characters you have recently chatted with, stored to populate your sidebar.
  • Usage data: Token counts, session start times, and weekly usage totals, used to enforce plan limits and display your usage dashboard.
  • Subscription tier and PayPal subscription ID: Your current plan (Free, Advanced, X20, or X50) and, for paid subscribers, the PayPal subscription identifier linked to your account, stored in our database.
  • Content moderation records: Per-conversation violation strike counts (for our hate speech and slur policy) are stored to enforce our 3-strike moderation system. These records are tied to your account and character pair, not to your individual messages. Starting a new chat with a character resets the strike count for that character.
  • User-created characters: Any characters you create, including their name, description, system prompt, and associated image.

Automatically collected:

  • Session identifiers (stored in a browser cookie, used for authentication)
  • Basic server logs (request timestamps, error messages) for operational purposes
  • Your IP address — used solely for geographic location estimation during in-chat crisis detection (see Section 3a below). It is never stored in our database and is not linked to your account or profile.
  • Browser GPS location (optional) — if you grant permission when opening the Resources panel, your device's GPS coordinates are used for a one-time reverse-geocode lookup to display locally relevant support resources. Coordinates are never stored. See Section 3a for full details.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session
  • To deliver AI character responses tailored to your conversation history
  • To save and display your archived past conversations through the in-app History panel

  • To process subscription payments via PayPal and send email receipts after successful charges

  • To detect and prevent abuse or violations of our Terms of Service

3a. Location Data and Geographic Resources (Safety Features)

Character.Mind uses location information in two ways to provide locally relevant crisis and support resources. Both methods are used solely for safety purposes and neither stores your location data in our database.

Method 1 — Browser GPS (Resources panel)

When you open the Resources panel (the heart icon in the sidebar), your browser asks for permission to share your device's GPS or network-based location. This permission request is initiated by your browser and is entirely optional.

  • What we collect: If you grant permission, your device's latitude and longitude coordinates are sent to our server over HTTPS. We pass these coordinates to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim reverse-geocoding service to determine your city, province/state, and country.
  • What we do not do: We do not store your GPS coordinates or the resulting location in our database. We do not link your location to your account or profile. We do not use it for advertising, tracking, or any purpose other than displaying locally relevant support resources.
  • How long it is kept: Coordinates are used for a single reverse-geocode lookup and immediately discarded. They are never written to disk or our database.
  • If you deny permission: Your browser location is not accessed. We fall back to IP-based geolocation (described below) to estimate your region.
  • Third party: Your coordinates are sent to Nominatim (openstreetmap.org) to determine your location. Only the coordinates are shared; no account information is transmitted. OpenStreetMap's privacy policy applies to data they process.

Method 2 — IP Address (in-chat crisis detection)

When our automated system detects a message that may indicate personal distress or thoughts of self-harm, we perform a one-time, ephemeral lookup of your IP address to estimate your geographic region. This is done solely to direct you to the Resources panel with locally relevant support lines.

  • What we look up: Country, province/state, and city, derived from your IP address via a third-party geolocation service (ip-api.com).
  • What we do not do: We do not store your IP address in our database. We do not link your IP or location to your account or profile. We do not use it for advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than safety resource delivery.
  • How long it is kept: The result is cached in server memory for up to one hour to avoid repeated external lookups, then discarded automatically. It is never written to disk or our database.
  • Accuracy: IP geolocation is approximate. It identifies a general region, not a precise address. If you have already granted GPS permission via the Resources panel, that more accurate location is used instead.
  • Third party: Your IP address is sent to ip-api.com to determine your approximate region. Only your IP is shared; no account information is transmitted. ip-api.com's privacy policy applies to data they process.

Legal basis for both methods: Under PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25, and GDPR, this processing is justified by vital interests — the safety of a person who may be in crisis — and by our legitimate interest in providing accurate, life-relevant information.

If you are uncomfortable with location detection, you may deny the GPS permission prompt and use a VPN. Safety resources will still be displayed; they may be less region-specific.

4. Third-Party Services

We share data with the following third parties only as necessary to operate the service:

  • Google OAuth 2.0 — handles sign-in. We receive your basic profile information as described above. Google's privacy policy applies to data processed by Google.
  • Groq — our AI inference provider. Your conversation messages are sent to Groq's API to generate character responses. If you attach an image, it is also sent to Groq so we can check it against our content rules and, if it passes, so the character can respond to it. Groq's privacy policy applies to data they process.
  • ElevenLabs — text-to-speech provider, used only when voice calls are available. The text of a character's reply is sent to ElevenLabs to generate the spoken audio. No account information is transmitted. ElevenLabs' privacy policy applies to data they process.
  • Neon (PostgreSQL) — our database host, where user data, conversations, and character definitions are stored.
  • Render — our hosting provider, where the application server runs.
  • PayPal — payment processing for paid subscriptions. PayPal's privacy policy applies to payment data they collect.
  • ip-api.com — geographic location service. When crisis language is detected in a message, your IP address is sent to ip-api.com to determine your approximate region so we can display locally relevant crisis resources. Only your IP is shared; no account information is transmitted. ip-api.com's privacy policy applies to data they process.
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap — reverse-geocoding service. When you open the Resources panel and grant GPS permission, your device's coordinates are sent to Nominatim to determine your city, province, and country. Only your coordinates are shared; no account information is transmitted. OpenStreetMap's privacy policy applies to data they process.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Data Retention

  • Active conversation history is retained while your account is active and may be cleared at any time from within the app.
  • Archived past conversations are retained while your account is active and are browsable from the History panel. They are included in account deletion requests.
  • Content moderation records (strike counts per user-character pair) are retained while your account is active to enforce our policies. Starting a new chat with a character resets the count for that character.
  • Usage data (daily/weekly counters) is retained for the current billing and limit window, then reset.
  • Account data (name, email, subscription tier, PayPal subscription ID) is retained as long as your account exists. The PayPal subscription ID is removed when a subscription is cancelled, expired, or suspended.
  • IP geolocation results are cached in server memory for up to one hour and are never stored in our database. See Section 3a.
  • If you request account deletion, we will remove your personal data from our database within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.
