Section 01

Overview

Character Mind Playtime Co ("we," "us," or "our") operates Character.Mind. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the service, you agree to the practices described here.

Section 02

Information We Collect

From your Google account (at sign-in):

Name & profile picture Google email address Unique Google account ID

From your use of the service:

  • Active conversation history: Messages exchanged with AI characters are stored server-side to maintain continuity across sessions. You can clear this at any time from within the app.
  • Archived past conversations: When you start a new chat with a character, the previous conversation is automatically saved to your archive so you can browse it later through the History panel. Archived conversations are stored in our database and are included in account deletion requests.
  • Long-term memory notes: When you chat with Opys or Opys 5, we keep a short, automatically written summary of the earlier part of that chat so the character can remember it. It is stored with your account, used only for that chat, and deleted when you start a new chat or clear it.
  • Data kept in your browser: To load quickly, your device keeps a copy of your recent chats, past chats, profile picture, display name and preferences in your browser's local storage. Signing out does not erase it. To remove it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Recent chats: A list of characters you have recently chatted with, stored to populate your sidebar.
  • Usage data: Token counts, session start times, and weekly usage totals, used to enforce plan limits and display your usage dashboard. This includes how many minutes of voice calls you have used today.
  • Character relationship data: For each character, we store how many messages you have sent them (your friendship level), whether they have given you their in-app phone number, and any temporary block (when it ends and how many times they have hung up on you). This powers the Phone contacts, friendship and hang-up features. In-app phone numbers are fictional and are generated from your account ID; they are not real phone numbers.
  • Voice calls: We do not record or store call audio. Your speech is converted to text by your browser's built-in speech recognition — depending on your browser this may be processed by the browser maker (for example, Google for Chrome) under their own privacy policy. The resulting text is sent to our servers and handled like a chat message, and the character's reply text is sent to a text-to-speech provider (see Third-Party Services) to create the spoken audio.
  • Subscription tier and PayPal subscription ID: Your current plan (Free, Advanced, or X20) and, for paid subscribers, the PayPal subscription identifier linked to your account, stored in our database.
  • C.M. Coins balance: Your virtual coin balance and daily coin-earning activity, stored in our database. Coins have no monetary value and cannot be withdrawn.
  • Push notification subscription: If you enable push notifications, your browser's push subscription endpoint and encryption keys are stored in our database so we can deliver notifications to your device. We also store the name of the character you last spoke with and a summary of the last message context (used to personalize re-engagement reminders). You can revoke this permission in your browser settings at any time. Stale subscriptions (those that return a 410 or 404 error) are automatically deleted from our database.
  • Group chat data: When you use group chat, the conversation history (character names, messages, timestamps) is stored in server memory for the duration of your session. Group chat history is not permanently persisted to our database and is cleared when the session ends or after a period of inactivity (4 hours). You may have up to 50 group conversations stored at a time.
  • Wellness check-in preferences (optional): If you opt in to wellness check-in messages, we store your preference (on/off), your chosen frequency, and optionally a phone number you provide. The phone number is stored solely for future voice-call features; it is not used for any other purpose. You can update or remove these at any time from Settings → Wellness.
  • Content moderation records: Two types of moderation data are stored on your account: (1) per-conversation violation strike counts for our hate speech and slur policy, tied to your account and character pair; and (2) an account-level real-world violence strike count and, when applicable, a temporary ban expiry timestamp. These records exist solely to enforce our moderation policies and are not linked to your individual message content.
  • User-created characters: Any characters you create, including their name, description, system prompt, and associated image.

Automatically collected:

  • Session identifiers (stored in a browser cookie, used for authentication)
  • Basic server logs (request timestamps, error messages) for operational purposes
  • Your IP address — used solely for geographic location estimation during in-chat crisis detection (see Section 3a). It is never stored in our database and is not linked to your account or profile.
  • Browser GPS location (optional) — if you grant permission when opening the Resources panel, your device's GPS coordinates are used for a one-time reverse-geocode lookup to display locally relevant support resources. Coordinates are never stored.

Section 03a

Location Data and Geographic Resources (Safety Features)

Character.Mind uses location information in two ways to provide locally relevant crisis and support resources. Both methods are used solely for safety purposes and neither stores your location data in our database.

Method 1 — Browser GPS (Resources panel)

When you open the Resources panel (the heart icon in the sidebar), your browser asks for permission to share your device's GPS or network-based location. This permission request is initiated by your browser and is entirely optional.

  • What we collect: If you grant permission, your device's latitude and longitude coordinates are sent to our server over HTTPS. We pass these coordinates to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim reverse-geocoding service to determine your city, province/state, and country.
  • What we do not do: We do not store your GPS coordinates or the resulting location in our database. We do not link your location to your account or profile. We do not use it for advertising, tracking, or any purpose other than displaying locally relevant support resources.
  • How long it is kept: Coordinates are used for a single reverse-geocode lookup and immediately discarded. They are never written to disk or our database.
  • Third party: Your coordinates are sent to Nominatim (openstreetmap.org). Only the coordinates are shared; no account information is transmitted. OpenStreetMap's privacy policy applies to data they process.

Method 2 — IP Address (in-chat crisis detection)

When our automated system detects a message that may indicate personal distress or thoughts of self-harm, we perform a one-time, ephemeral lookup of your IP address to estimate your geographic region.

  • What we look up: Country, province/state, and city, derived from your IP address via ip-api.com.
  • What we do not do: We do not store your IP address in our database. We do not link your IP or location to your account or profile.
  • How long it is kept: The result is cached in server memory for up to one hour to avoid repeated external lookups, then discarded automatically. It is never written to disk or our database.
  • Third party: Your IP address is sent to ip-api.com to determine your approximate region. Only your IP is shared; no account information is transmitted.

Legal basis for both methods: Under PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25, and GDPR, this processing is justified by vital interests — the safety of a person who may be in crisis — and by our legitimate interest in providing accurate, life-relevant information.

If you are uncomfortable with location detection, you may deny the GPS permission prompt and use a VPN. Safety resources will still be displayed; they may be less region-specific.

Section 04

Third-Party Services

We share data with the following third parties only as necessary to operate the service:

  • Google OAuth 2.0 — handles sign-in. We receive your basic profile information as described above.
  • Google Gemini API — our primary AI provider. Your conversation messages (including what you say on voice calls, as text) are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate character responses; images you attach are sent to it for safety checks and replies; and the text of a character's reply is sent to it to create spoken audio for voice calls.
  • Groq — our backup AI provider. When the primary provider is busy, your conversation messages are sent to Groq's API to generate the character's response instead.
  • Microsoft Edge TTS / Google Translate TTS — alternative text-to-speech providers used for voice calls and read-aloud. The text of a character's reply is sent to these services to generate spoken audio. No account information is transmitted.
  • Neon (PostgreSQL) — our database host, where user data, conversations, and character definitions are stored.
  • Render — our hosting provider, where the application server runs.
  • PayPal — payment processing for paid subscriptions and C.M. Coin purchases.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery. Your email address and the content of service emails (receipts, wellness check-ins, platform updates) are transmitted to Resend to be delivered. Resend does not use this data for advertising.
  • ip-api.com — geographic location service used during crisis detection. Only your IP is shared; no account information is transmitted.
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap — reverse-geocoding service. When you open the Resources panel and grant GPS permission, your device's coordinates are sent to Nominatim to determine your city, province, and country.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Section 05

Data Retention

  • Active conversation history is retained while your account is active and may be cleared at any time from within the app.
  • Archived past conversations are retained while your account is active and are browsable from the History panel. They are included in account deletion requests.
  • Content moderation records — per-conversation hate speech strikes and account-level real-world violence strikes and ban expiry — are retained while your account is active to enforce our policies.
  • Usage data (daily/weekly counters) is retained for the current billing and limit window, then reset.
  • Character relationship data (friendship levels, earned phone contacts and block history) is retained while your account is active so your progress with each character is kept. Blocks end automatically when their time is up. This data is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Account data (name, email, subscription tier, PayPal subscription ID) is retained as long as your account exists. The PayPal subscription ID is removed when a subscription is cancelled, expired, or suspended.
  • IP geolocation results are cached in server memory for up to one hour and are never stored in our database. See Section 3a.
  • If you request account deletion, we will remove your personal data from our database within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Section 06

Email Communications, Wellness Check-ins & Push Notifications

Service emails: By creating an account, you agree to receive service-related emails such as important policy updates and account notices. These are not marketing emails. We do not send promotional emails without your explicit opt-in.

Wellness check-ins: If you opt in to wellness check-ins (Settings → Wellness), the character you chat with most will periodically send you a personal email and push notification to check in on how you are doing. These messages are sent on the schedule you choose (every 3 days, weekly, or every two weeks). Every check-in email includes a reference to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. You can turn this off at any time from Settings → Wellness.

Push notifications: If you enable push notifications in your browser, we may send you real-time notifications including wellness check-ins, platform announcements, and activity alerts. You can revoke push notification permission in your browser settings at any time.

To stop receiving service emails, contact us at support.charactermind@gmail.com. Note that opting out of service emails may mean you miss important updates about your account or the platform.

Section 08

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Character.Mind is intended for users who are 13 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect, use, or disclose personal information from children under 13.

Age verification: We rely on self-declaration at account creation. When you sign in, you represent that you are at least 13. We do not currently use automated document or biometric age verification. While we acknowledge that self-declaration can be circumvented, it constitutes the platform's reasonable effort to screen underage users under COPPA. Users who misrepresent their age to gain access do so in violation of our Terms of Service, and responsibility for that misrepresentation lies with the user or their parent or guardian.

If a child under 13 registers: If we become aware — through a report, a support request, or our own discovery — that a user is under 13, we will immediately suspend the account and delete all associated personal data, including conversation history, profile data, usage records, and any coins or subscription credits. No refunds will be issued for fraudulently obtained access.

Parents and guardians: If you believe your child under 13 has created an account on Character.Mind, contact us immediately at support.charactermind@gmail.com. We will verify and delete the account and all associated data within 5 business days.

Character.Mind does not serve advertising, and we do not sell or share data with third parties for marketing purposes. This limits the risk posed to any minor who gains access, but it does not change our policy: the service is for users 13 and older.
