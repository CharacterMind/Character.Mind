2. What We Don't Collect

  • We don't collect payment information (there is no paid tier).
  • We don't sell your data to anyone.
  • We don't run ads or share data with advertisers.
  • We don't track you across other websites.

3. IP Address & Geolocation

If your message contains language that suggests you may be in personal distress (such as expressions of suicidal thoughts or self-harm), the Service temporarily uses your IP address to determine your approximate country and region. This is used to provide the AI with locally relevant crisis resources — for example, the correct crisis line for your country.

Your IP address is sent to ipapi.co, a third-party geolocation service. We receive only your country and region — not a precise location. The result is cached in server memory for up to one hour and is never written to a database. ipapi.co's own privacy policy governs their handling of your IP.

This geolocation lookup only happens when crisis-related keywords are detected. It does not happen during normal conversations.

4. Message Analysis & Content Moderation

Before your messages are forwarded to the Gemini AI, the Service analyzes them in server memory to detect prohibited content (such as hate speech, explicit sexual content) and safety-related language (such as expressions of distress). This analysis:

  • Happens entirely in server memory — it is not logged or stored in a database.
  • May result in the message being deflected with a canned response rather than sent to Gemini.
  • Tracks a per-conversation warning count (for repeated slur use) in server memory only; this counter is not written to any database and is cleared when the conversation ends.
  • Triggers the geolocation lookup described in section 3 if crisis-related language is detected.

Messages that pass this check are sent to Gemini to generate a response. The content of your conversations is not retained by us beyond your active session.

5. Third-Party Services

The Service relies on the following third parties:

  • Google OAuth — for sign-in. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini API — your conversation messages are sent to Google's Gemini AI to generate responses. Google's data handling policies apply to these requests.
  • Neon (PostgreSQL) — your characters are stored in a Neon-hosted database. Conversation history is held in server memory only and is not written to the database.
  • Cloudflare — the Service is delivered through Cloudflare's network. Cloudflare may process request metadata per their privacy policy.
  • Netlify — the site is hosted on Netlify's infrastructure.
  • ipapi.co — used for geolocation in crisis detection only (see section 3).

6. Cookies & Sessions

We use a single session cookie to keep you signed in. This cookie is essential for the Service to work and is not used for tracking or advertising. It expires after 7 days or when you sign out, whichever comes first.

7. Age Requirement

The Service is intended for users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete their data.

8. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete your characters at any time from within the app.
  • Clear your conversation history by starting a new chat.
  • Sign out to end your session.
  • Request deletion of your account data by emailing us (see below). We will delete your characters and associated data within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Data Transfers

The third-party services we use (Google, Neon, Netlify, Cloudflare, ipapi.co) are based primarily in the United States. By using the Service, you consent to your data being processed there. If you are located in Quebec, please be aware that your information may be transferred to and processed in jurisdictions outside Quebec.

10. Data Security

We use HTTPS for all data in transit. Your data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database with access controls. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. If we make significant changes, we'll update the "last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the Service after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
