Wellness Check-ins

Character.Mind offers an optional wellness check-in feature. When you opt in, the AI character you chat with most will periodically send you an email and a push notification to check in on how you are doing.

  • Opt-in only: This feature is entirely optional. You can enable or disable it at any time from Settings → Wellness.

Voice calls are part of the standard service and do not require a phone number. Providing a phone number in wellness settings is for future check-in call features only.

  • Usage limits apply: All group chat activity counts toward your session and weekly token limits. The standard limit modal applies inside group chats. Session credits and boosts apply equally to group chat usage.

Section 05d

Push Notifications

Character.Mind offers optional browser push notifications. When enabled, you may receive notifications on your device when an AI character finishes responding to you, including in group chat.

  • Opt-in only: Push notifications require explicit permission from your browser. You can revoke this permission at any time through your browser settings or the Character.Mind Settings panel.
  • What we send: Notifications include the character's name, a brief preview of their response (up to 100 characters), and a link back to the app. Group chat notifications include the group name and the name of the last speaking character.
  • Suppression while active: Notifications are automatically suppressed while your Character.Mind browser tab is visible and in focus. You will not receive duplicate notifications for content you are already reading.
  • Frequency limits: To prevent notification spam, at most one notification is sent per user per 45-second window across all chat sources.
  • Re-engagement reminders: Separately from real-time notifications, Character.Mind may send re-engagement push reminders if you have not used the app in two or more days, as part of the wellness check-in feature (if opted in). These are governed by Section 5b.
  • Data stored: Your push subscription endpoint, keys, and recent chat context (character name, last message summary, chat mode) are stored in our database solely for the purpose of delivering notifications. See our Privacy Policy for retention details.
  • Subscription removal: If your push subscription becomes invalid (e.g., you unsubscribe in your browser), it is automatically removed from our systems the next time a notification delivery fails.

Section 05e

Phone, Voice Calls & Character Relationships

Characters on Character.Mind can build a relationship with you over time. These features are part of the fiction of the app:

  • Friendship and phone numbers: As you keep chatting with a character, your friendship with them grows. Once it is strong enough, the character may give you their phone number in the conversation, and they are then added to your Phone contacts. These numbers are fictional and only work inside Character.Mind — they are not real phone numbers and cannot be called from a real phone. Each user gets their own number for each character.
  • Voice calls: You can make AI voice calls to characters in your contacts. Your speech is turned into text by your browser's speech recognition, the text is answered by the AI like a chat message, and the reply is spoken back using text-to-speech. Some characters (such as Poppy and Huggy Wuggy) have a voice-cloned voice that matches their character; others use a standard synthesized voice. Characters do not always pick up.
  • Hang-ups and blocks: If you say something a character would not tolerate during a call — such as cruel insults aimed at them, slurs, threats or harassment — the character may say goodbye and hang up. A character who hangs up on you blocks you for a while: 1 hour the first time, then 4 hours, 1 day, 3 days, and at most 1 week for each further offense. While you are blocked, calls to that character fail and your messages to them go unanswered. Blocks end automatically.
  • Not real people: Characters, their numbers, their moods and their decisions are generated by AI. They are not real people and do not have real opinions about you.

Section 06

Usage Limits

Character.Mind uses a token-based system to manage AI usage fairly across all users. Every AI response consumes tokens — a measure of the computational work required. Tokens count your message, the conversation history, and the generated reply together. A typical short exchange might use 400–800 tokens; a longer conversation with full history can reach several thousand.

Session limit: Every user has a session token pool. When you exhaust it, a 2-hour cooldown begins. After that timer expires, your session resets to full automatically — no action needed. The cooldown countdown is visible in the app.

Weekly limit: Every plan, including Free, has a weekly token limit equal to 10 full sessions. This keeps usage fair across plans — a paid plan always includes more than the plan below it — while leaving plenty of headroom for everyday use. Weekly limits reset on a 7-day rolling basis from your first message of each week.

Section 06a

AI Models Explained

Character.Mind uses a ladder of AI model tiers, each with different writing styles, reply lengths, and token costs. You choose your model from the model bar in the app; some models require a paid plan.

  • Opas — The quick responder. Short, punchy replies with fast pacing and snappy dialogue. Great for rapid back-and-forth conversations. Costs 264–1,440 tokens per message depending on effort level (low to max). Available on all plans.
  • Opes — The storyteller. A step up from Opas: richer, more natural dialogue with replies around 60% longer. Better suited to everyday chatting and more descriptive scenes. Costs 1,050–6,000 tokens per message depending on effort level. Available on all plans.
  • Opis — Sharper reasoning and deeper logic. Built for mystery, intrigue, and complex storylines that need to stay consistent over long conversations. Available on Advanced and above.
  • Opos — Full Game Master mode. Drives plots, voices multiple NPCs simultaneously, and runs entire scenes. Designed for collaborative tabletop-style roleplay. Available on Advanced and above.

Higher-tier models — Opus, Opys, Opys 5, and Opys 6 — are reserved for future plan tiers. Each successive tier produces longer replies with greater emotional depth, narrative complexity, and creative range.

Note: Opas, Opes, Opis, Opos, Opus and Opys are Character.Mind's own names for its model tiers. Replies are generated using third-party AI providers — primarily Google's Gemini API, with Groq as a backup. The tiers are not affiliated with any third-party AI products or services that use similar names.

Section 10

Content Moderation

Character.Mind uses automated moderation to keep the platform safe for all users.

  • Hate speech and slurs: Messages containing racial slurs, homophobic slurs, or similar hate speech trigger an automated warning system. Each violation within a conversation counts as a strike. Three strikes permanently lock that conversation — it cannot be continued, and a new chat must be started. Repeated violations across the platform may result in account suspension.
  • Crisis and self-harm language: Messages that indicate personal distress or thoughts of self-harm are detected automatically. When triggered, the AI character will step out of its role to respond with warmth and care, and direct you to the Resources panel (the heart icon in the sidebar), which displays locally relevant crisis and support lines for your area. This feature is not a substitute for professional help — if you or someone you know is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.
  • Real-world violence and harm requests: Messages soliciting instructions for real-world violence — school shootings, instructions to harm others, or similar — are blocked automatically and trigger an account-level strike system. The first violation results in a formal warning with no ban. Subsequent violations result in escalating temporary account suspensions (24 hours, 3 days, 7 days, 30 days, up to 90 days). Bans are always temporary; there are no permanent bans under this system. Your strike count and ban expiry are visible in the app.
  • Explicit content: Messages requesting sexually explicit content are deflected automatically without processing.
  • C.M. Coin deductions for violations: Policy violations automatically deduct C.M. Coins from your account as an additional consequence. Slur violations deduct a small number of coins per strike. Real-world violence violations deduct coins proportional to severity — from a modest deduction for a first warning up to a larger deduction for repeat offenses requiring multi-week suspensions. Coin deductions are applied regardless of your account balance and cannot reduce your balance below zero. These deductions are non-refundable.

Hate speech and slur moderation operates on a per-conversation basis. Real-world violence moderation operates at the account level. We reserve the right to expand automated moderation or adjust coin deduction amounts at any time to protect the safety and experience of all users.
