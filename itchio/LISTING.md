## Description (paste this)

**character.mind** is a website where you chat with AI characters, or create your own.

**Open it with the button above.** It opens in a new tab.

**What it does**
- Chat with ready-made characters (Poppy, Lily Lovebraids and Doey so far), each with their own personality and voice, or build your own with a personality and picture
- Six models, each with its own job: Opas (quick chat), Opes (everyday stories), Opis (light coding, puzzles and mysteries), Opos (game master and scene director), Opus (chapter writer that remembers how your story began), Opys (master author with long-term memory and every ability)
- Five effort levels: Low is a few quick sentences, Medium one or two paragraphs, High a full scene, Extra long and planned before it is written, Max chapter-length and checked after it is written
- Sign in with Google. Your chats stay with your account
- A free plan, with paid plans for the heavier models

**Please know**
- It is early and built by one person, so there will be bugs
- The free server sleeps when nobody is using it, so the first load can take up to a minute
- This project uses generative AI for its chat text and its code
- The Poppy Playtime characters are fan-made and inspired by the game; Poppy Playtime belongs to Mob Entertainment
- Explicit content is not allowed
- Voices and calling your AI are coming soon

**Feedback and bug reports:** r/CharacterMind on Reddit (use the Bug Report flair) or use "Contact us" inside the site.
